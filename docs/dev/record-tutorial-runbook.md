# Record a procedure in Document Studio

Use this checklist when filming the **how to record a tutorial** guide in Chrome. Auto-logged steps only fire on the WW360 tab running Record tutorial — not in Firefox, another browser, or an external app.

## Before you start

1. **Chrome only.** Sign in as an author (`jenny-oww` or your admin account).
2. Open **Content → Document Studio**.
3. Switch to **WW360 platform library** (scope `program:{state}`) if the library switcher is available.
4. Select the **Getting started** or **Tutorials** folder.

## Recording settings

1. Click **Record tutorial**.
2. Mode: **Screen + mic** (use **Screenshots only** if video is blocked).
3. Click **Start**. When the browser asks what to share, pick **This tab** (the WW360 Chrome tab) for the richest auto steps.
4. Click through the on-screen steps below.
5. When the **second** share picker would appear (Window / Entire screen / another app), **show it but do not pick another app**. Speak the closer, then click **Stop**.
6. Review: fix captions, delete accidental recorder-chrome steps, keep 5–7 clear steps. Generate or edit the written guide. **Save**, then **Publish**.

If you need a clip that shows an external app, film that separately (entire screen) and attach it as extra video — do not expect auto steps from it.

## Voiceover (45–60 seconds)

> Welcome to Document Studio. This is how you record a procedure so someone else can follow it.
>
> Open Record tutorial. Choose Screen plus mic for most walkthroughs. Screenshots only is there if video is blocked.
>
> Click Start. When the browser asks what to share, pick this Water Workforce tab if you are documenting the app itself. Pick a window or the entire screen if the work happens in another program.
>
> From that point, do the real job in whatever you shared. Each click in Water Workforce 360 becomes a step. Clicks in another program are not logged — say what you did, or use Capture frame.
>
> When the procedure is done, click Stop. Fix the step titles. Save or submit so your manager can publish it into the library.

## On-screen steps (should auto-log)

1. Document Studio is open (opening step).
2. Click **Record tutorial**.
3. Choose **Screen + mic**.
4. Click **Start** (first share picker: **This tab**).
5. Point at the floating recorder bar — **Stop** and **Capture frame** live here.
6. Optional: mention Window vs Entire screen for an outside app (do not switch apps).
7. Click **Stop**. Review the step list.

## Written guide closer (edit after generate)

> After you share a window or the entire screen, carry out the procedure in that application. Water Workforce 360 records the video and any clicks you make here. It cannot see clicks inside the other program — add those steps by hand or with Capture frame. Then stop, review, and publish.
